# CascadeEWS 🌊

**CascadeEWS: A Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Network with 
Physics-Informed Cascade Propagation Index for Multi-Tipping 
Early Warning in Climate Systems**

Author: Haarika Alla
Manipal Institute of Technology, Bengaluru
Submitted to: Climate Dynamics (Springer) — Q1 Journal

---

## What This Project Does

CascadeEWS detects climate tipping points — sudden collapses 
in systems like Arctic sea ice, AMOC ocean currents, and Amazon 
rainfall — before they happen. Unlike all prior methods that 
watch one location at a time, CascadeEWS models the entire 
Earth as a connected graph and detects how instability spreads 
from one region to another (cascade propagation).

Result: AUC-ROC 0.919 | 6.1 weeks advance warning | 
        Outperforms all 5 baselines including Bury et al. 2021

## Datasets Used

1. SYNTHETIC CLIMATE DATA (primary — generated by code)
   - 703 global grid nodes at 10 degree resolution
   - 40 years weekly = 2,080 timesteps
   - SST, Sea Ice, Precipitation fields
   - ENSO (4.2yr), PDO (22yr), AMO (70yr) signals
   - 20,352 tipping events with CSD precursor injection
   - Generated by: cascadeews_train.py (Step 1)

2. NSIDC ARCTIC SEA ICE INDEX (real-world validation)
   - Monthly Arctic sea ice extent 1979-2024
   - Used for Kendall-tau CSD validation on real data
   - Free download, no login required
   - URL: https://nsidc.org/data/g02135
   - Auto-downloaded by: cascadeews_train.py (Step 3)

3. BERKELEY EARTH TEMPERATURE ANOMALY (auxiliary features)
   - Global monthly surface temperature anomaly
   - Rohde & Hausfather (2020) Earth Syst. Sci. Data
   - URL: https://github.com/datasets/global-temp

4. OUR WORLD IN DATA CO2 + CLIMATE (auxiliary features)
   - CO2 concentration and climate forcing indicators
   - Friedlingstein et al. (2023) Earth Syst. Sci. Data
   - URL: https://github.com/owid/co2-data

5. OUR WORLD IN DATA ENERGY (auxiliary features)
   - Global energy transition data
   - Ritchie et al. (2022) Nature Energy
   - URL: https://github.com/owid/energy-data

## Four Original Contributions

1. TELECONNECTION GRAPH
   703 nodes + 5 literature-validated edges:
   ENSO→Indian Ocean, ENSO→N.America, ENSO→W.Africa,
   AMO→Sahel, PDO→Arctic
   First climate tipping paper to use hard-prior physics edges.

2. PHYSICS-INFORMED CASCADE LOSS (PICL)
   L = Σ w_h · L_focal + λ · ReLU(τ_AC − p_mean)
   First neural network loss to embed CSD bifurcation theory.
   Contributes +0.042 AUC over standard focal loss.

3. CASCADE PROPAGATION INDEX (CPI)
   CPI_v = Σ A_uv · p_u · exp(−d_uv / 2000km)
   New metric measuring incoming cascade risk from neighbours.
   Gives warning before local indicators show anything.

4. MC-DROPOUT UNCERTAINTY MAPS
   50 stochastic passes → spatially resolved confidence maps.
   Identifies which regions need more monitoring.
